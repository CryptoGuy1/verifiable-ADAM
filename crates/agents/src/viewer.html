How was this checked?

The team needs two matching signed votes. The coordinator collects them but does not vote. The checker then checks the signatures, the readings, the test rule and whether the readings are still recent enough. A majority can be wrong.

These agents ran on one computer using public test keys and simulated readings. They are not three independent devices. Readings expire after 30 seconds; a round has a 20-second deadline. This demo uses an initial equal-weight rule, not the full original ADAM policy. Live Pi operation and private proofs (ZKP) are not included.
